Pet Financial Assistance & Support

Canadian Pet Support Network

Making veterinary care more accessible for pets across Canada.

🐾 Find Help for Your Pet

Finding affordable veterinary care and pet support can be difficult. We've gathered organizations and resources across Canada to help keep pets healthy and with their families.

Browse the resources below to find veterinary assistance, affordable services, pet food programs and emergency support.

Please note: Programs, eligibility, funding availability and services can change. Contact each organization directly to confirm current details.

❤️ Adopted Your Pet From a Rescue?

Before applying elsewhere, contact the rescue organization you adopted your pet from. Many rescues have their own assistance programs, veterinary partnerships or may be able to help with unexpected medical expenses.

Alberta Helping Animals Society (AHAS)

Veterinary assistance

Provides compassionate veterinary services in Edmonton to qualified clients who cannot access regular veterinary care.

Veterinary financial assistance

National Pet Care Fund

National veterinary assistance

Helps low-income Canadian pet owners cover part of the costs of non-elective veterinary medical treatments and procedures for companion animals.

Parachutes for Pets

Food and crisis support

Offers pet food assistance, essential supplies through Tito's Treasures, and temporary safekeeping for pets while families face medical emergencies, domestic violence or housing instability.

Compassionate Care Program

Veterinary assistance

Assists qualifying Alberta families with diagnosing suspected ailments and necessary veterinary treatment when no other options are available. Not available for emergency assistance.

Central Alberta Humane Society

Offers low-income spay/neuter assistance through its PALS program and free short-term emergency boarding for people experiencing a crisis. Also offers pet food support.
